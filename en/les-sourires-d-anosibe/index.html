The Smiles of Anosibe

Portraits of children in Antananarivo, alongside the charity MealEspoirs

On 13 October 2026, during a stopover in Antananarivo, I was lucky enough to spend a morning with the charity MealEspoirs, which helps the children of the Anosibe slum, some of whom are orphans.

The ritual is simple: the children arrive, wash their hands, share a meal, brush their teeth, and then set off again.

They are growing up amid the hardship of a slum, some of them without their parents. And yet every one of them arrived with a smile, and posed for these portraits with a joy and a zest that I will not forget.

All profits from the sale of prints of these portraits go to MealEspoirs.

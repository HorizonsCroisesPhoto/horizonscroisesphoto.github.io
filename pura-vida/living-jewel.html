Living Jewel

Green-crowned Brilliant, Costa Rica

Chaque plume est une écaille d'émeraude : un bijou vivant, immobile le temps d'un souffle.

A female green-crowned brilliant pauses for an instant, her iridescent feathers catching the light like tiny emerald scales. A rare moment of stillness from a bird that never stops moving.

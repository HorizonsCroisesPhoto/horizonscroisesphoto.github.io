Painted Souls

Street art photographed in Bogotá, San Francisco and Miami

In Bogotá, San Francisco and Miami, the walls tell the story of the city better than any guidebook: giant faces, musicians, eyes that follow you round the street corner.

This series brings together those painted encounters. I photograph each work just as I find it, in the light of the moment and with the life around it: an overhanging branch, a street sign, the marks of time on the wall.

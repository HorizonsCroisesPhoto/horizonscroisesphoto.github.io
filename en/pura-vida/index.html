Pura Vida

Wildlife photography in Costa Rica

In Costa Rica, the forest never reveals itself all at once: a flash of yellow in the foliage, a wingbeat too fast for the eye, a spotted silhouette melting into the shadows.

This series brings together four close-up portraits, taken during two stopovers in San José, in October 2025 and January 2026. Almost unreal colours, and gazes that give each one its own personality.

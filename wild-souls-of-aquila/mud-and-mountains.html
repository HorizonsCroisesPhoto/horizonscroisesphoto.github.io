Mud & Mountains

White Rhino, Aquila

Vêtu de boue séchée, un rhinocéros blanc traverse le fynbos doré, portant la montagne sur son dos.

Still coated in fresh mud, a white rhino strides through the golden fynbos with the Western Cape mountains rising behind it. It's a timeless image of one of Africa's most iconic and endangered animals.

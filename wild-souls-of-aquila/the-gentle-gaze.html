The Gentle Gaze

Giraffe Portrait, Aquila

Du haut du Karoo, une girafe incline son regard vers vous, comme si toute la savane retenait son souffle.

A close, face-to-face portrait of a giraffe at Aquila Private Game Reserve near Cape Town. Soft backlight outlines its ears and ossicones, and a single droplet hangs from its muzzle. A calm, curious presence for any wildlife lover's wall.

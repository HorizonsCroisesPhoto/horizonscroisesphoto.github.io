The Wanderer

African Elephant, Aquila

Sortant de l'ombre dans les herbes dorées, un vieil éléphant avance lentement, portant la lumière comme un souvenir.

An African elephant steps out of the golden grass and looks straight at the lens, with a dark hillside behind it. Every wrinkle, dust mark and weathered tusk tells a story. This is a powerful vertical portrait of the gentle giant of the Karoo.

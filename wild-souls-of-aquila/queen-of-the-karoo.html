Queen of the Karoo

Lioness Yawn, Aquila

Tête renversée, gueule grande ouverte, une lionne lance sa voix vers le ciel, et le Karoo tout entier en frémit.

Head thrown back and jaws wide open, a lioness reveals her long canines in a dramatic yawn. Captured in sharp profile against a soft background, this vertical print radiates raw power and presence.
